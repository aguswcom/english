title: Past Tense :Jawa Timur - The Legend of Mount Kelud
level: Pemula
grammar: Simple Past Tense
---
Long ago, a beautiful princess named Dewi Kilisuci lived in the kingdom of Kediri. Many kings wanted to marry her, so they sent gifts to the palace.

Two strong kings, Mahesa Suro and Lembu Suro, loved her very much. They had the heads of a buffalo and a cow, but they were also very proud.

The princess did not want to marry them. She thought of a clever plan and said, "Dig a deep well on top of the mountain for me."

The two kings agreed and worked all night. They dug the well with their strong hands, and they wanted to finish before morning.

When the well was ready, the princess asked them to go inside. They climbed down, and then the soldiers threw stones into the well.

The kings shouted in anger, but they could not get out. Before they died, they said, "One day, we will take revenge on the people of Kediri."

After that, the people of Kediri made small offerings every year. They remembered the story, and they stayed careful near the mountain.

Moral: Pride and greed can bring trouble to ourselves.
