---
title: Past Tense :Jawa Timur - The Legend of Surabaya
level: Pemula
grammar: Simple Past Tense
---
Long ago, a strong shark named Sura lived in the sea. A big crocodile named Baya lived in the river nearby.

Both animals were very strong, and both of them loved to eat fish. The sea and the river were full of fish, so they had enough food.

One day, Sura swam into the river and caught a big fish. Baya saw him and shouted, "Stop! This is my place, and the fish are mine!"

Sura felt angry and said, "I found this fish first. It is my food!" Then they started to fight.

They fought for many days, and the water turned red. Both animals were tired and hurt, but neither of them won.

At last, Sura said, "This fight is silly. The sea is my home, and the river is yours." Baya agreed, and they made a promise.

From that day, Sura stayed in the sea, and Baya stayed in the river. They never fought again, and the place of their fight became known as Surabaya.

Moral: Peace is better than fighting, and sharing makes everyone happy.
