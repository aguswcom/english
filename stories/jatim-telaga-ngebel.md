Long ago, in a village in Ponorogo, East Java, there lived a farmer and his wife. They were very sad because they did not have any children.

One day, the wife went to a sacred place. She prayed for a child. A few months later, she got pregnant. The couple was very happy.

When the baby was born, they were shocked. The baby was not a normal child. She was a dragon. The mother cried, but she still loved her daughter. She named her Baru Klinting.

Years passed. Baru Klinting grew bigger and bigger. She lived in the forest near the village. She was lonely, but she always helped the villagers.

One day, the villagers held a big ceremony. They invited everyone to a feast. Baru Klinting also wanted to come. She said, "Mother, may I join the feast?" Her mother said, "Yes, but please be careful."

Baru Klinting went to the feast. But the villagers laughed at her. They said, "A dragon cannot join us!" They did not give her any food.

Baru Klinting was very sad. She went back to the forest. She prayed to God. Suddenly, a big storm came. Rain fell for many days. The village was flooded. The water formed a big lake.

The lake is now called Telaga Ngebel. People say the lake came from the tears of a dragon who was hurt by the villagers.

Kind people are always welcome.
