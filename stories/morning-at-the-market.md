---
title: Morning at the Market
level: Menengah
---

The market opens at six, but Daniel always arrives before the first customers. He sets up his stall carefully, placing the ripest tomatoes at the front where people can see them. His grandfather taught him this trick many years ago.

By seven o'clock, the street is full of noise. Sellers shout their prices, children weave between the crowds, and the smell of fresh bread drifts from the bakery on the corner.

Today, a woman stops at his stall and picks up a tomato. "Are these local?" she asks. Daniel nods proudly. "From a farm twenty kilometers away. I picked them up yesterday afternoon." She buys two kilos and promises to come back next week.

Not every day is so easy. When it rains, fewer people come, and some vegetables spoil before they are sold. Still, Daniel loves the work. He likes talking with regular customers, and he enjoys the quiet moment at noon, when he finally sits down with a cup of tea and watches the street slow down.
