---
title: The Lost Key
level: Menengah
---

Maya searched her bag for the third time. Her phone, a notebook, half a sandwich, but no key. She was already ten minutes late for work, and the rain was getting heavier.

She tried to stay calm and retraced her steps. That morning she had made coffee, fed the cat, and answered a call from her sister. Then she had rushed out of the door, hoping nothing was forgotten.

A neighbor, Mr. Lopez, was watering his plants under the porch roof. "Lost something?" he asked. Maya sighed and explained. He laughed kindly and pointed at her jacket. "Have you checked your pockets?"

She had not. Maya slid her hand into the left pocket and felt something cold and metal. The key had been there the whole time.

"Thank you," she said, feeling a little embarrassed. Mr. Lopez shrugged. "It happens to everyone. Last week I looked for my glasses for an hour, and they were on my head."

Maya locked the door, opened her umbrella, and hurried to the bus stop, smiling despite the rain.
