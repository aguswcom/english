---
title: The Last Bus
level: Menengah
---

Sara missed the nine o'clock train, so she decided to take the night bus home. The stop was almost empty, lit by a single flickering lamp. She checked the timetable twice. The next bus was due in fifteen minutes.

When it finally arrived, the driver greeted her with a tired smile. Only three other passengers sat inside: an old man reading a newspaper, a student wearing headphones, and a woman asleep against the window.

Sara chose a seat near the back and watched the city pass by. Shop windows were dark, and the streets were almost silent. She felt strangely peaceful, as if the whole town belonged to her.

Halfway through the journey, the bus stopped suddenly. A cat was sitting in the middle of the road, calmly washing its paw. The driver waited, chuckled, and honked softly until the animal wandered away.

"Happens every night," he told Sara through the mirror. "That cat thinks the road is hers."

Sara laughed. By the time she reached her stop, she realized she no longer minded missing the train.
