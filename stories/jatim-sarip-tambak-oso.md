Long ago, a brave boy named Sarip lived in a village near Surabaya. His father died when he was a baby, so he lived with his mother.

Sarip was poor, but he was strong and kind. He worked hard every day and helped his neighbors.

At that time, a greedy landlord ruled the area. He took rice from the poor farmers and gave them nothing.

One day, the landlord's men came to Sarip's house. They took his mother's rice and laughed at her.

Sarip felt angry, so he went to the landlord's house. He asked for the rice back, but the landlord shouted at him.

The landlord's men attacked Sarip, but he fought bravely. He won and brought the rice back to the villagers.

The farmers cheered and thanked him. They called him a hero, and they were happy again.

After that, the landlord became afraid and stopped his bad deeds. The village lived in peace.

Moral: Courage and kindness can defeat injustice.
