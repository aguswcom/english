![](jatim-suminten-edan.webp)
Long ago, a kind girl named Suminten lived in a village in Java. Her father was a poor farmer, and her mother died when she was young.

Suminten worked hard at home every day. She cooked, cleaned the house, and took care of her father.

One day, a rich man named Pak Cakrawangsa saw Suminten and liked her. His son, Raden Cakrawangsa, married her, and they moved to a big house.

The rich man's family was not kind to Suminten. Her stepmother and stepsister were jealous, so they made a bad plan.

They put a strange herb in her food. Suminten ate it and felt dizzy, and then she began to act in a funny way.

People in the village thought she was crazy and called her Suminten Edan. Her husband felt sad, but he still loved her.

He took her to a wise healer, and the healer gave her special medicine. Slowly, Suminten became healthy again.

The bad people felt ashamed and said sorry. Suminten forgave them, and the family lived happily.

Moral: Do not be jealous of others, and always forgive people who say sorry.
