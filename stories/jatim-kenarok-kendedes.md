---
title: Past Tense :The Legend of Ken Arok and Ken Dedes
level: Pemula
grammar: Simple Past Tense
---
Long ago, a poor young man named Ken Arok lived in East Java. He was strong and smart, but he had no home and no family.

One day, he met a wise priest. The priest said, "You will become a great king one day, so work hard and be brave."

Ken Arok went to the kingdom of Tumapel and worked for the ruler, Tunggul Ametung. The ruler was a rich man, and he had a beautiful wife named Ken Dedes.

One day, Ken Arok saw a bright light around Ken Dedes. A priest told him, "The man who marries her will become a great king."

Ken Arok wanted to change his life, so he made a secret plan. He took a special dagger and defeated the ruler.

After that, Ken Arok became the new ruler of Tumapel, and he married Ken Dedes. The people accepted him because he was strong and clever.

Later, he fought the king of Kediri and won the battle. He built a new kingdom and named it Singasari.

Moral: Dreams can come true, but we must always choose good ways to reach them.
