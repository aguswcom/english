![](stories/jatim-gunung-bromo.webp)
Long ago, a kind princess named Roro Anteng lived in the Majapahit kingdom. She married a strong young man named Joko Seger, and they moved to the mountains near Tengger.

They built a small village and became its leaders. The villagers loved them, and the village was peaceful.

But the couple was sad because they had no children. They climbed the mountain every day and prayed to the gods for a baby.

One day, a loud voice came from the smoke of the mountain. It said, "I will give you many children, but you must give me your youngest child."

The couple agreed, and soon they had twenty-five children. Their youngest son was named Kesuma.

Roro Anteng and Joko Seger loved Kesuma very much, so they did not keep their promise. The gods became angry, and fire came out of the mountain.

Kesuma was afraid, but he was brave. He jumped into the hot crater and said, "Please keep the villagers safe."

The mountain became calm again. Every year, the Tengger people remembered Kesuma and threw rice, fruit, and flowers into the crater.

Moral: A promise is important, and we must always keep it.
